## 15 Nov (Spark, bench print)
"The two pieces on the head of the probe, the one that mounts the servo, one of the mounting pillars with the hole in it broke off. This thing has like zero support. We need to reprint this and redesign it as well."
One of the SG90 tab posts (the pillar with the screw hole) snapped off the printed head frame. Redesign so the servo mount cannot break: every load the posts really see (the tab screws driven in and tightened, the servo's stall torque and its mass in crashes, handling), the posts' layers along their bending, posts rooted into the body, not standing alone.
